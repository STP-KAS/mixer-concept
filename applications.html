What these applications establish

These are experimental, unaudited contracts on Testnet-10. The browser connects directly to a community node. The faucet only sends test coins to your address; it never receives your wallet keys.

All example roles belong to this tab. Treasury signatures demonstrate a threshold, not independent people approving. The prediction oracle chooses an outcome; it does not verify an outside event. Proof payout verifies a published fixture, not a private computation you created.

Timed actions use the connected node’s median time. Release and refund can race; the first accepted spend wins. A missing submission response remains uncertain and blocks new spending until matching outputs are observed. Closing the tab does not cancel a transaction.
