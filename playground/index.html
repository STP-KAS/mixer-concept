What you are looking at

        
The live network is a blockDAG: honest miners can find blocks at the same time, and Kaspa keeps them. Bitcoin throws the extra honest work away. Ethereum left proof of work. These five models are that story, slowed down. They do not move your tKAS unless you use Send above.

      

      

        

        
News is late
Two miners, a delay, parallel blocks. This is why Kaspa is not a chain.
Same coins twice
One output, two attempts. Speed does not cancel the ledger check.
Payment, change, fee
One input becomes payment, change, and a fee. Same as Bitcoin’s coin model.
Many blocks, uneven pay
The network can hum while your miner finds almost nothing. Variance is not a bug in the DAG.
Spending rules
A withdrawal with three conditions. Toccata on L1, not a smart-contract chain.
