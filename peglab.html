    What this is, and what it is not
    

      
This page runs the PegLab economic engine in your browser. The live lab with KasWare is at /lab/. Source: STP-KAS/peglab-stp. Use it as a benchmark: if your dapp “needs a stable,” run this failure mode first. There is no business model here. Anybody can compile a similarly named series. A name is not authenticity. Verify genesis outpoint, template hash, series bytes, and backing.
