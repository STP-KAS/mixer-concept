Assumptions, redemption rights, and Treasury backing

This invented issuer values its Treasury assets at par. The model does not sell securities, estimate bank settlement, charge fees, or model insolvency. Only paid redemptions reduce the token supply. Moving the slider starts a new snapshot.

A real token needs enforceable redemption terms and custody arrangements. On-chain arithmetic cannot establish that assets exist, are unencumbered, or are legally owed to you. Tokenized Treasury funds also depend on their fund documents, access rules, and intermediaries.

Calculation and missing infrastructure

Health factor = collateral value × 0.75 ÷ debt. These invented parameters illustrate one lending rule. There is no interest, price impact, fee, or liquidation execution here. A real system needs price feeds, available buyers, and rules for shortfalls. Code cannot guarantee those will work during a crash.

Claims, resolution, and model boundaries

One unit of collateral backs one complete Yes/No pair. Only one side receives the unit after binary resolution. Before resolution, a complete pair could be merged back into collateral. This example shows settlement, not trading, odds, fees, disputes, or forecasting quality. Pending does not mean a 50/50 payout.
